Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Social Manager is a private tool used by a marketing agency to schedule and publish content to the social media accounts of its clients, with their permission. This policy explains what data the tool handles.

Data we access

  • When an account owner connects Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or LinkedIn, we receive an access token and basic profile information (account name, ID and picture).
  • Content that our team uploads to publish: text, photos and videos.
  • Performance numbers that the platforms provide for published content (views, reach, interactions).

How we use it

Only to publish the content the account owner approved, on the schedule they chose, and to show them reports about it. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted. Data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. Uploaded videos are deleted automatically about 7 days after publishing.

Removing access

An account owner can disconnect at any time from the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services), or by asking us. When an account is disconnected we delete its tokens.

Contact

For any question or to ask for your data to be deleted, contact the agency that manages your account.
